# Visual Guide: All-in-One Ecosystem

This guide provides a detailed breakdown of every section in the app, using the current UI architecture as a reference.

---

## 1. Executive Dashboard (Home)
The heart of your productivity system. It provides a real-time "Aura" that reflects your current state.

- **Dynamic Aura**: The header gradient changes based on your **Mood Log**.
- **Safe Spend**: A quick look at your monthly budget minus expenses.
- **Pulse Activity**: A horizontal scrolling feed of your most recent completions.
- **Section Cards**: Interactive entry points for Habits, Workouts, Tasks, and more.

---

## 2. Growth & Discipline (Habits & Workouts)
Focused on long-term physical and mental improvement.

### Habit Tracker
- **Streak System**: Maintains momentum via a specialized streak algorithm.
- **Frequency Sorting**: Filter rituals by Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
- **Grace Days**: A safety net for your streaks when life gets busy.

### Workout Routine
- **Active Mode**: Specialized UI for tracking repetitions and sets.
- **Muscle Recovery**: Automatic 48-hour tracking for specific body parts (Chest, Back, etc.).
- **Volume Math**: Calorie burn is calculated based on the tracking mode (Timer vs Reps).

---

## 3. Management & Notes (Tasks, Notes, Projects)
Organizing your day-to-day work and future roadmaps.

### Smart Tasks
- **Priority Categorization**: Segment your life into 'Work', 'Personal', and 'Shopping'.
- **Auto-Archive**: Keeps your workspace lean by hiding completed items after 7 days.

### Project Roadmaps
- **Sub-Features**: Break down massive goals into small, actionable nodes.
- **Change History**: A detailed log of every milestone achieved.
- **Synergy Sync**: Automatically links project goals to your main task list.

### Notes & Templates
- **Privacy Mode**: Hide sensitive information using the 'Hidden' toggle.
- **Daily Gratitude**: Pre-filled templates to make journaling frictionless.

---

## 4. Financial Vault
Track your cash flow and budget targets.

- **Independent Ledgers**: Separate your personal spending from shared expenses or lending.
- **Target Analysis**: Visualization of spending vs. monthly budget caps.

---

## 5. Identity Hub (Profile)
Your personal progression record.

- **XP & Leveling**: Gain ranks as you complete your daily rituals.
- **Data Governance**: Export your entire ecosystem to JSON for local backups.
- **App Lock**: Biometric and PIN-based security for your data.
