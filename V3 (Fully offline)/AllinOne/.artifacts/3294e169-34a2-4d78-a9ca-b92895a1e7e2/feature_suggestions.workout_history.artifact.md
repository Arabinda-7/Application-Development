# Workout History Feature Suggestions

The current Workout History provides a solid foundation with volume-weighted heatmaps, muscle radar charts, and readiness gauges. To elevate it further into a "Scientific Training Partner," here are several feature suggestions grouped by their primary benefit.

## 1. Achievement & Milestone Tracking
*   **🏆 Personal Best (PB) Hall of Fame**: A dedicated section to view your "Top Records" for each exercise (e.g., Max Weight for Bench Press, Fastest 5k).
*   **✨ New Record Badges**: Days in the calendar grid could show a small "sparkle" or badge if a new PR was achieved that day.
*   **🏅 Consistency Trophies**: Unlock virtual medals for milestones like "30-Day Streak," "100 Workouts Finished," or "Leg Day Veteran."

## 2. Advanced Physiological Analytics
*   **🧘 Energy vs. Intensity Correlation**: Allow users to log "Energy Levels" (1-5) before a workout. Visualize how pre-workout energy correlates with total volume or intensity.
*   **🔥 Caloric Burn Velocity**: A chart comparing "Workout Duration" vs "Estimated Calories Burned" to identify the most "efficient" workout types for weight loss.
*   **📈 Estimated 1-Rep Max (1RM) Trend**: For strength exercises, use formulas (like Brzycki) to calculate and chart your estimated 1RM over time, showing strength progression without testing maxes.

## 3. Visual & Social Engagement
*   **📸 Transformation Photo Journal**: Attach "Progress Photos" to specific days in the workout history. A "Comparison Slider" tool would allow users to see visual changes between two selected dates.
*   **🎨 Training Type Distribution**: A pie chart showing the percentage breakdown of your workouts (e.g., 50% Strength, 30% Cardio, 20% Mobility).
*   **📊 Monthly Summary Cards**: Generate a shareable "Infographic" summarizing the month's achievements (Total weight lifted, hours spent, muscle group focus).

## 4. Intelligent Coaching & Goals
*   **🎯 Monthly "Missions"**: Set specific goals (e.g., "Squat 2,000kg total volume this month" or "15 Cardio sessions"). Show a progress bar towards these missions in the history view.
*   **🧠 "Weak Point" Alerts**: AI-driven insights like: *"You haven't trained 'Back' in 8 days. Your volume for this group is 20% lower than your average."*
*   **🕒 Time-Block Density**: A heatmap showing which *times of day* you are most likely to complete a workout successfully.
