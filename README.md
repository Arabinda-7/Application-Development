All in One: Your Personal Productivity Ecosystem
All in One is a holistic life-management application for Android, designed to centralize your daily workflows. From tracking your finances and fitness to managing complex projects and capturing fleeting ideas, this app serves as your digital headquarters.

🚀 Key Features

📅 Daily Planning & Productivity
• Task Management: Organise your day with categorical To-Do lists and subtasks.
• Habit Tracker: Build and maintain consistency with visual progress tracking.
• Note Taking: A versatile digital notebook for all your thoughts and references.
• Universal Search: Instantly find any task, note, or project milestone from a single search bar.

💼 Project & Knowledge Management
• Project Boards: Manage complex projects with milestones and sub-features.
• Idea Mind Maps: Visualize your thoughts and branch out your creative ideas.
• Architect Tree: A structured way to view your project hierarchies.

💰 Financial Wellness
• Personal Ledger: Keep track of transactions and debts with a dedicated ledger book.
• Budgeting: Monitor your monthly expenditure vs. budget with "Safe to Spend" insights.
• Finance Heatmaps: Visualize your spending patterns over time.

🏋️ Health & Mindfulness
• Workout Routines: Track your fitness journey with detailed routine logs and adapters for various exercises.
• Mood Tracking: Log your daily mood and receive AI-driven growth and management advice.
•Performance Dashboard: A bird's-eye view of your total daily progress across all life sectors.

🎨 Highly Customizable Experience
• Adaptive UI: Supports Light, Dark, and true OLED modes.
• Glassmorphism Design: Beautiful glass-style cards with adjustable blur and transparency.
• Theming: Deep customization of accent colors, border radii, and typography (Serif, Monospace, etc.).
• Focus Modes: Adjust home screen display sizes (XS to L) to suit your needs.

🛠 Tech Stack
• Language: 100% Kotlin
• UI Framework: Jetpack Compose (Modern, declarative UI)
• Design System: Material 3
• Architecture: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
• Data Persistence: Custom DataManager for robust state and local storage management.
• Concurrency: Kotlin Coroutines

📂 Project Structure

com.example.allinone
├── activities/       # Core feature Activities (Finance, Habit, Workout, etc.)
├── adapters/         # RecyclerView adapters for legacy/hybrid components
├── ui/               # Compose-based UI (HomeScreen, PerformanceDashboard)
├── data/             # Data models and central DataManager
└── utils/            # UI and Logic helpers (Swipe gestures, Icon previews)

🏃 Getting Started
1. Clone the repository:

 git clone https://github.com/your-username/AllinOne.git
2. Open in Android Studio: Ensure you have the latest version of Android Studio (Koala or newer).
3. Build & Run: Connect an Android device or emulator (API 26+) and press Run.
